Jira

This runs acli jira auth login --web, which opens the Atlassian login in your browser. Clearsky stores no Jira credentials.

Google Calendar

Option A, no new login: add your work Google account in your desktop's Settings → Online Accounts → Google, with Calendar switched on. Enter your work email below and save. Clearsky reads the calendar GNOME syncs.

Add a personal Google account in Online Accounts (Calendar only) to see family appointments here too. On a shared calendar (one not named after your address, like "Family"), events only take your time when you're invited, or when one of these names is in the title; the rest just show in the agenda.

Option B, iCal link (only if your admin allows it). You can use both; meetings are merged:

  1. Open Google Calendar settings.
  2. Under Settings for my calendars, click your calendar, then Integrate calendar.
  3. Copy Secret address in iCal format and paste it below.

Slack

  1. Create the Clearsky Slack app (pre-filled; pick your work workspace).
  2. Click Install to Workspace. Your admins may need to approve it.
  3. Under OAuth & Permissions, copy the User OAuth Token (xoxp-…) and paste it below.
