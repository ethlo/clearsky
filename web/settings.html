Plugins

Everything Clearsky reads from. Open one to set it up.Each page also shows what the plugin holds and reaches. One you add from a git repo, npm or a folder runs in a sandbox of its own: it reaches only the hosts it lists, keeps files only in its own folder, and gets only its own settings. You see what it reaches before any of its code runs.

Clearsky loads plugins when it starts.

From GNOME Online AccountsNo new login: add your work Google account in your desktop's Settings → Online Accounts → Google, with Calendar switched on. Enter your work email below and save. Clearsky reads the calendar GNOME syncs.

Or a Google iCal linkOnly if your admin allows it. You can use both; meetings are merged.
  1. Open Google Calendar settings.
  2. Under Settings for my calendars, click your calendar, then Integrate calendar.
  3. Copy Secret address in iCal format and paste it below.

Keeps Copilot to your folders and the hosts below.

Credits and costThe Usage page adds up the AI credits every chat used, from Copilot's own session logs, and shows what they cost. Credits become dollars at the price below (check your Copilot plan), and dollars become your currency at your rate. With the live rate on, Clearsky looks the rate up once a day from the European Central Bank's public rates (frankfurter.dev, no account or token); if that fails it uses your rate.
