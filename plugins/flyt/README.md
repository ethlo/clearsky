# Visma Flyt Skole for Clearsky

School messages, notices, forms and weekly plans for your children, from Visma Flyt Skole (Min Skole / Flyt Foresatt). Add it with `clearsky add flyt` or under Settings → Plugins, then fill in **School portal key** and click **Log in with BankID**. See the School section of Clearsky's README for what it does.

It runs inside Clearsky rather than in a sandbox of its own, because the login opens a browser window and the session is kept in your keyring.
