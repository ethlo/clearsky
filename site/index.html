Yours, on your machine

It runs on 127.0.0.1 with the logins you already have, such as gh. No tokens to make, no server, no account. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Sorted by whose move it is

One list instead of five tabs: what needs you now, your tickets, and what you're waiting on. Done hides an item until something new happens on it.

Your agent, in a sandbox

Copilot or Claude Code works in your clone, fenced to your folders. A push, a ticket change or a new host waits until you click Allow.

Works with what you already use

Clearsky doesn't pick your tools for you. Choose on the welcome page or in Settings, change your mind any time, and let a team pack set it for everyone.

Work

Every review, PR, ticket and mention in one list

GitHub, your tracker and plugins such as Slack in one place, grouped by what each one needs from you. Each row says the next step: review it, read the new commits, fix the failing checks, reply.

  • Bots don't count as reviewers, and threads you answered last aren't yours
  • Knows which code in your PRs you haven't read yourself
  • J K to move, E done, H snooze, Ctrl K to find anything
Agent chat

A coding agent on any PR or ticket

Start a chat from any row and it opens in the right clone or worktree, with the PR or ticket attached. It can run long builds in the background, have a fresh reviewer look at its change before it opens a PR, and keep notes you approve.

  • Background jobs that wake the chat when they're done
  • A self-review against the ticket before the PR
  • Writes comments and PR descriptions the way you do
Stand-up

Your stand-up, already written

Yesterday, today and blockers from what you actually did: PRs opened and reviewed, tickets moved, what your agent did for you. Copy it wherever your team posts it, or let the agent tidy it up.

Next to it, Now / Next shows your next meeting, how long you're free, and work that fits the gap.

A sandbox that asks before anything leaves

Clearsky doesn't try to judge shell commands. Your agent runs inside a sandbox that keeps it to your folders and allowed hosts, and a few doors lead out. Your GitHub login never enters the sandbox.

Plugins and teams

Add what else you use. Share a setup with your team.

Slack, Confluence, Grafana and school messages are plugins, each running in a sandbox of its own that reaches only the hosts it lists. You see what a plugin reaches before any of its code runs.

A team pack is a small repo your team owns: organizations, stand-up time, links, skills and pinned plugins. Colleagues join with one command, and company details never go into Clearsky itself.

Up in a minute

You need Node 22 and git. clearsky try shows everything with made-up data first, so you can look around before you log in to anything.

Getting started guide 
git clone https://github.com/ethlo/clearsky
cd clearsky
sh scripts/install.sh   # never uses sudo

clearsky try            # made-up data, no logins
clearsky serve          # http://127.0.0.1:7777
